03 / ACCURACY

Calibrate drawing scale

Scans are measured in pixels, not feet or meters. Calibration establishes how many drawing units each processed pixel represents.

  1. Select the correct sheet and enter the dimension value under Known distance.
  2. Choose its unit (feet, inches, meters, millimeters, or centimeters).
  3. Click Pick two points, zoom in, and click point A.
  4. Pan or zoom as necessary, then click point B. The app stores a scale for that sheet.
  5. Export and compare a second independent dimension in CAD.
Not georeferencing

Two-point calibration sets a measurement scale. It does not establish survey control, coordinate-system location, rotation, or real-world georeferencing.

04 / PROCESSING

Conversion settings

The Studio interface keeps advanced options under Convert → Advanced conversion. The classic interface shows more controls directly in the Convert tab.

Resolution

96 DPI is faster, 150 DPI is a good first pass, and 200–240 DPI can recover finer scan details. Higher settings increase processing time.

Threshold and noise

Adaptive threshold helps with faded or uneven backgrounds. Speckle removal cleans small isolated marks but can also erase tiny valid features.

Recognize circles

Attempts to fit real CAD CIRCLE entities to sufficiently complete round strokes or matching fragments. Uncertain circular geometry stays as linework.

Straighten lines

Reduces small scanning distortions using best-fit lines. Optional orthogonal snapping affects only nearly horizontal or vertical lines; keep it off for drawings with intentional slopes.

Hatch recognition

Detects repeated line patterns inside reliable closed regions and creates CAD HATCH entities. Tiny border gaps can be repaired automatically; larger gaps need manual selection.

Dashed and hidden lines

Looks for regularly spaced collinear dash strokes and assigns DASHED or HIDDEN CAD linetypes. Classification reflects graphic rhythm, not proven engineering meaning.

Text and watermark

Optional OCR can convert printed labels to CAD text; verify every reading. PDF-XChange trial-overlay filtering is targeted and may not detect all watermarks.

05 / QUALITY

What becomes editable CAD?

The vector preview shows the reconstructed CAD geometry, not just a processed black-and-white image. Recognition is still experimental.

LINE / LWPOLYLINE

Traced centerlines and path geometry

CIRCLE

Fitted center and radius

HATCH

Diagonal, crosshatch or solid fill

DASHED / HIDDEN

Pattern-aware line entities

TEXT

Recognized PDF text and optional OCR

Review every sheet

Look for missing lines, duplicate traces, open intersections, misidentified dashed patterns, circular details that should be arcs, and incorrect hatch boundaries. Use AutoCAD or Civil 3D to validate before engineering use.

06 / REPAIR TOOLS

Repair a hatch boundary

For sections with damaged or missing hatch borders, the app can create a manually bounded native HATCH without reconverting the entire source sheet.

  1. Convert the sheet first. Open Review & scale → Hatch editor in Studio, or the manual hatch controls on the classic Review ribbon.
  2. Choose Use source pattern, Diagonal lines, Crosshatch, or Solid fill.
  3. For a manually chosen line pattern, specify its angle and spacing in processed pixels. Start near the spacing visible in the original scan.
  4. Click Draw hatch boundary and select three or more corners on the drawing. Use the wheel to zoom or hold Space and drag to pan.
  5. Click Finish hatch or press Enter. Use Undo point while drawing or Undo hatch afterward to revert the most recent manual addition.
Replace matched source strokes

This option is off by default. Enable it only if the region contains true hatch strokes that should be removed from the remaining geometry. Otherwise the original strokes can remain behind the newly created HATCH.

07 / DELIVERY

Layers and export

Use the Layers tab to name the linework, text, and hatch layers. You can choose a drawing discipline preset and override detected dash groups to all DASHED or all HIDDEN.

DXF

Download DXF

Exports the selected converted sheet as editable CAD entities. Studio also provides a quick DXF button in the header after conversion.

ZIP

Batch ZIP

Downloads DXFs for completed sheets plus a QA-manifest.json with calibration, processing, and geometry-recognition diagnostics.

DXF, not DWG

Native DWG output and intelligent Civil 3D objects such as alignments, surfaces, and pipe networks are not supported. Open the DXF in AutoCAD or Civil 3D and save a DWG there if required.

08 / QUESTIONS

Troubleshooting

My converted drawing is missing thin lines. What should I change?

Try 200 DPI instead of 150 DPI, then Adaptive threshold if the source is faded. Reduce speckle cleanup and the minimum line length cautiously. Reconvert and compare in Overlay and Vectors only.

Why are some curved objects made of segments?

True CIRCLE fitting is limited to round features with enough visible evidence. An arc, ellipse, partial circle, or heavily obscured feature may remain a polyline. Confirm shape and radius manually in CAD.

Why did a dashed utility line become a hidden line?

The classifier recognizes a pattern of marks, not the engineering meaning. Use the detected linetype override in Layers for the selected sheet, then verify the result in CAD.

Why didn't my hatch become a HATCH entity?

The automatic detector needs a reliable closed boundary and consistent repeated strokes. For incomplete section borders, use Review & scale → Hatch editor (Studio) to select a polygon and choose a pattern.

The scale is wrong after exporting. What should I check?

Confirm you selected the correct units, clicked the exact known endpoints, and measured a substantial distance. Recalibrate the sheet and check a second independent dimension in CAD. Calibration does not supply survey coordinates.

Why is a large PDF taking so long?

Large engineering sheets are processed tile by tile. Choose 150 DPI first, crop to the required drawing area, and disable OCR if you only need geometry. High DPI and OCR cost more CPU and memory.

Can I export a DWG or Civil 3D surface?

Not directly. Export DXF, open and check it in AutoCAD or Civil 3D, and save as DWG if needed. The app does not create parametric Civil 3D objects.

Does the converter work offline?

Conversion runs in your browser after the required code has loaded, but the app and some dependencies are hosted externally. Initial loading and optional OCR language-model downloads can require a network connection. Offline availability is not guaranteed.

09 / SECURITY

Privacy and limitations

The converter is designed to process files locally in the browser. No application endpoint uploads your selected PDF, image, or generated DXF for conversion.

Your drawing processing stays local

Libraries and optional OCR models can still be downloaded over the internet. Avoid adding confidential project files to public GitHub issues, shared links, or screenshots.

AsBuilt Vectorizer is an experimental drawing-reconstruction tool. Source quality, print scaling, skew, line joins, OCR, and recognition heuristics affect accuracy. The output is not survey-georeferenced, engineering certified, or a substitute for professional CAD review.
